Frequently asked questions

What does a small business website need?

At minimum: what you do, who you serve, proof such as photos or reviews, and easy ways to contact you. Extra pages can be added later.

Will people find my business on Google?

The site is built with local SEO basics. Ranking takes time, and I also guide you on setting up Google Business Profile, which matters a lot for local searches.

Can I start small and add features later?

Yes. I build the site so pages and features such as booking or an online store can be added later.
