Frequently asked questions

How long does a business website take?

A typical 5–8 page business website takes 1–3 weeks from content approval to launch. Web applications take longer depending on features; I give a clear timeline before we start.

Will my website appear on Google?

Every site I build includes technical SEO: clean page structure, meta tags, sitemap, robots.txt, structured data and fast loading. I also help you register with Google Search Console so your pages get indexed.

Do you build mobile apps as well?

Yes. I build Android and iOS apps with React Native and Expo, and connect them to the same backend as your website.

Who will I be working with?

Directly with me. I am an IIT Kharagpur graduate and a full-stack developer, so you talk to the person who actually builds your product.

Can you maintain the site after launch?

Yes. I can handle hosting, domain setup, updates and new features after launch.
